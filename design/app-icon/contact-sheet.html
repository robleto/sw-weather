Galactic Weather — app icon concepts

Each concept shown at display size, then at 60 pt (home screen), 40 pt (Settings) and 29 pt (notifications), over light, dark and wallpaper-like backgrounds. Corners are rounded for preview only — the shipped asset is a full-bleed square.

A — Binary Dawn · limb + two suns

Deep-space field over the app's own #05070A, a planet's curved limb across the lower third with a cool rim glow, and two suns that are unmistakably a pair. Keeps the warm palette you liked but sets it against black, so it reads off-world instantly and doesn't compete with every other orange gradient on the home screen.

B — Two Suns · your icon, repaired

The closest thing to what you have: same warm sky, but the second sun is promoted to a true twin, the suns go white-hot for real contrast against the orange, the dunes are gone, and the cloud has a fully rounded base instead of the hard horizontal cut. Still the most generic of the four — this is the "keep the direction, fix the craft" option.

C — Ringed World · strongest silhouette

A ringed planet with a lit limb and a distant sun. The ring gives it the most distinctive outline of the four, which is what actually buys you recognition at 29 pt in a notification stack. Says "another world" with total clarity — but says nothing about weather on its own.

D — Storm Eye · best concept fit

A banded world filling the frame with a visible storm system and a rim light on the lit limb. This is the only one of the four that depicts weather and another planet in a single form, which is exactly the app's pitch. One big shape plus one focal feature is also the composition that downscales best.

None of these are shippable assets yet. They're vector concepts for picking a direction — flat gradients, no painterly texture, and nothing matching the hand-illustrated quality of the planet art in the app. Once you pick one, it wants a real illustrator pass (or an image model) to get the texture, and then a flattened 1024×1024 PNG: no alpha, no rounded corners, no baked shadow.
